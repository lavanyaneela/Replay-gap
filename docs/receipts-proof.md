# Proof that the number checker fails when it should

A checker nobody has watched fail is a checker nobody should believe. This is the
transcript of seven deliberate corruptions, regenerated by tools/prove_receipts.sh,
and a test asserts that every case in it is a failure.

Cases 2 to 6 falsify a figure that is there. Cases 7 and 8 insert one that is not,
which is the attack the mechanism missed until an audit went looking for it: guarding
the known lines proves those lines are current and says nothing about a line beside
them.
