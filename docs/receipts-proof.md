# Proof that the number checker fails when it should

A checker nobody has watched fail is a checker nobody should believe. This is the
transcript of ten deliberate corruptions, regenerated by tools/prove_receipts.sh, and
a test asserts that every case in it is a failure.

Cases 2 to 6 falsify a figure that is there. Cases 7, 8, 10 and 11 insert one that is
not, in a fence, in a code span and in image alt text, which is the attack the
mechanism missed until an audit went looking: guarding the lines that are there proves
those lines are current and says nothing about a line beside them. Case 9 is the
subtlest, and it is the one that forced the anchor rule to become per occurrence rather
than per document: a sentence that inverts the finding while quoting the right number,
sitting a hundred lines from the sentence that states it correctly.
